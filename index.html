About Me

I am an undergraduate in Mathematics (Strengthening Basic Disciplines Program) at the School of Mathematics, Southeast University. My research focuses on LLM agents and reinforcement learning, with particular interests in credit assignment for long-horizon agent interactions, POMDP formulations, and agent harnesses. I aim to bring the formal reasoning skills developed through mathematics to the study of intelligent-agent algorithms.

Through research and competitions, I have gained end-to-end experience in problem formulation, algorithm implementation, experimental evaluation, and technical writing. I have led several mathematical modeling teams, coordinating responsibilities and schedules while taking primary responsibility for model design, implementation, and paper writing. I continue to pursue research on reinforcement learning and intelligent agents.

Outside research, I enjoy city walks, Go, and building interesting products with AI. I have also competed with Southeast University's dragon dance team at a Jiangsu provincial event. I hope to grow alongside outstanding peers, discovering the joy of research through collaboration and the creative possibilities of AI through practice.
